Prominent Native Indian Breeds Support Matrix

Your model dataset typically includes classification weights for these vital native breeds:

Gir (Cattle)

Distinct humped back, drooping ears, half-moon shaped horns. Native to Gujarat.

Sahiwal (Cattle)

Reddish-dun coat color, loose skin (Lola). Renowned heavy milk producer.

Murrah (Buffalo)

Jet black body with characteristic tightly curved spiral horns. Native to Haryana.

Jaffrabadi (Buffalo)

Massive frame, drooping horns curving upward. Heavy breed native to Gir forest.
